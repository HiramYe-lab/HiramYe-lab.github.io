Posted on 2026-02-11 Edited on 2026-02-12 In Deep Learning , PyTorch

Normalization layers are essential for stable training of deep neural networks. This post catalogs the key normalization modules available in PyTorch, with code and conceptual explanations.

LayerNorm

The reference document is here with the source code given here.

The expression is given by
$$ y=\gamma\frac{x-\mu}{\sqrt{\sigma^2+\varepsilon}}+\beta, $$
where μ and σ2 are the mean and variance, respectively, and γ and β are learnable parameters.

To clarify the deployment of μ and σ2, it is necessary to know how the samples are selected.

  • Assume input = torch.Tensor(a, b, c, d, e, f), that is input has 6 dimensions.
  • Assume normalized_shape = (c, d, e, f), that is normalized_shape has D = 4 dimensions.
  • normalized_shape is referred to as the realm where μ and σ2 are computed.
